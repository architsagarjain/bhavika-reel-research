## Rewrite rules (keep these, Claude follows them)

1. Keep the topic and the idea. Write every sentence fresh in my voice.
2. Never reuse the original creator's lines, jokes, stories or hook wording. Not even lightly reworded.
3. Use my own examples, my own results and my own products wherever I have them.
4. Spoken, not written: it should sound natural said out loud in one take.
5. Hook first. The first line has to stop the scroll on its own.
6. End with my call to action from above.
7. If the original makes a claim I can't back up myself, leave it out or swap in something I can prove.
